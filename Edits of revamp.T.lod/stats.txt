Stats Descriptions	Description
Might	"Might is the statistic that represents a character's overall strength, and his or her ability to put that strength where it counts.  Characters with a high Might statistic do more damage in combat, take less physical damage (Might bonus %, up to 40%) and resist Earth damage better."
Intellect	"Intellect represents a character's ability to reason and understand complex, abstract concepts. Maximum spell points for Elemental Magic adepts are based on Intellect. Highly intelligent characters better resist dispel and mana drain."
Personality	"Personality represents both a character's strength of will and personal charm.  Maximum spell points for Self Magic adepts and resilience to Mind and Spirit damage, curses, dispel and mana drain are based on Personality. More charismatic characters get better prices with Merchant skill."
Endurance	"Endurance is a measure of a character's physical toughness and durability. A high Endurance gives a character more hit points and resilience to Water and Body damage as well as more chances to avoid negative conditions inflicted by monsters such as Weak, Asleep, Drunk, Diseased, Unconscious and Aged."
Accuracy	"Accuracy represents a character's precision and hand-eye coordination.  A high Accuracy will allow a character to hit monsters and deal critical damage more frequently in combat and to intercept attempts to steal from him or her."
Speed	"Speed is a measure of how quick a character is.  A high Speed statistic increases a character's armor class, dodge chance (+0.5% per point of Speed bonus), the rate with which the character recovers from attacks and resilience to Fire and Air damage."
Luck	"Luck has a subtle influence throughout the game, but is most visible in the ability of a character to resist Light and Dark magical attacks and avoid taking (as much) damage from traps."
Hit Points	"Hit Points indicate how much damage your character can sustain before falling unconscious or dying.  A character is unconscious at 0 Hit Points or less, and dies when Hit Points reach -X, where X is current Endurance value.  The number in brackets is the Hit Points regained (or lost) per regeneration tick from all sources.  All Hit Points are restored after 8 hours of uninterrupted rest."
Spell Points	"Spell Points are needed to cast spells.  Every spell has a Spell Point cost that is deducted from this statistic when it's cast.  The number in brackets is the Spell Points regained per regeneration tick (Meditation and other sources).  Spell Points are restored after 8 hours of uninterrupted rest."
Armor Class	"Armor Class is a measure of the character's protection against physical attacks by armor worn.  HR Class (Hit Resistance Class) is the amount of damage a character can take without his or her armor being penetrated; it depends on the durability of the armor items. If armor is penetrated, its protection is reduced by the portion (Damage - HR Class) / Damage.  Phys (Physical Resistance) is the overall resistance to physical attacks, Might bonus included."
Evasion, Parry and Block	"Eva, Par and Blk are the chances to dodge, parry or block an enemy strike, depending on the equipment and the respective skills; only the best one is tried. Dodging evades damage of any kind but area spells and traps; parrying and blocking are 33% less effective against magic and 66% less against energy. Dodge chance grows with Speed bonus and Haste, drops when Weak, Afraid or Drunk; blunt blows are easier to dodge, projectiles harder."
Physical Damage Kinds	"Sla, Pie and Blu are the resistances to slashing, piercing and blunt physical damage: Phys combined with the armor type of every worn piece (body armor counts half, helm and boots 15% each, gauntlets and belt 10% each). Leather is good against slashing, weak to piercing; chain is strong against slashing, weak to piercing; plate resists slashing and piercing, weak to blunt. Grand Master in an armor skill removes the weakness."
Age	"Age shows the current age of your character.  The first number is the temporary age number, and the second is your real age.  Certain monsters and magical effects can raise your temporary age above your real age."
Level	"Level is a measure of the training your character has gone through.  A high level contributes to hit points and spell points."
Experience	"Experience is a simple indicator of your character's overall understanding of the world.  With enough experience points (and a little gold), you can train your characters in training grounds throughout the land to increase their level and gain skill points to spend on skills."
Attack Bonus	"Attack Bonus is the sum of all factors (skills, spells, Accuracy, etc.) that influence your character's chance to hit monsters with an equipped weapon."
Attack Damage	"Attack Damage is the range of damage your character does with an equipped weapon: all factors (Might, spells, certain weapon skills at expert or master, etc.) included, as well as the penalty of a worn or damaged weapon."
Shoot Bonus	"Shoot Bonus is the sum of all factors (skill, spells, Accuracy, etc.) that influence your character's chance to hit monsters with an equipped bow."
Shoot Damage	"Shoot Damage is the range of damage your character does with an equipped bow: all factors (spells, weapon bonuses, Might bonus, etc.) included, as well as the penalty of a worn or damaged bow."
Critical Strikes	"Crit shows the damage multiplier of a critical strike and, in brackets, the chance to strike critically, for melee and ranged attacks. Both depend on the weapon type, the weapon skill and mastery, Accuracy and certain enchantments."
Fire and Air	"Fire and Air Resistances lessen the damage taken from fire and air based attacks; Speed bonus adds to them.  The percent is the share of damage negated, the numbers are current / base resistance. Though a high resistance can greatly reduce the damage, it does not make your character immune. If the incoming damage is higher than base resistance value then the effectiveness of protection drops up to two times."
Water and Body	"Water and Body Resistances lessen the damage taken from water and body based attacks; Endurance bonus adds to them. Body Resistance also helps to avoid the deadly effects of poisoning, instant death and eradication.  The percent is the share of damage negated, the numbers are current / base resistance. If the incoming damage is higher than base resistance value then the effectiveness of protection drops up to two times."
Earth	"Earth Resistance lessens the damage taken from earth based attacks and helps to avoid being turned to stone; Might bonus adds to it.  The percent is the share of damage negated, the numbers are current / base resistance. Though a high resistance can greatly reduce earth damage, it does not make your character immune. If the incoming damage is higher than base resistance value then the effectiveness of protection drops up to two times."
Mind and Spirit	"Mind and Spirit Resistances lessen the damage taken from mind and spirit based attacks; Personality bonus adds to them. Mind Resistance also helps to resist insanity, paralysis and fear.  The percent is the share of damage negated, the numbers are current / base resistance. If the incoming damage is higher than base resistance value then the effectiveness of protection drops up to two times."
Light and Dark	"Light and Dark Resistances lessen the damage taken from light and dark magic; Luck bonus adds to them.  The percent is the share of damage negated, the numbers are current / base resistance. Though a high resistance can greatly reduce the damage, it does not make your character immune. If the incoming damage is higher than base resistance value then the effectiveness of protection drops up to two times."
Skill Points	"Skill Points are awarded whenever your character trains for a new level.  You can spend Skill Points on your skills at any time."
